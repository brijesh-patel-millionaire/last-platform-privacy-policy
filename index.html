Privacy Policy – Last Platform

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Developer: SRM Studio

Contact: srmstudio14@gmail.com

SRM Studio (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Last Platform (“the Game”).

Last Platform is a mobile arcade game in which players control balls, jump between platforms, collect gems, unlock different balls, and try to achieve high scores. The Game does not require users to create an account or provide registration information to play.

1. Information and services used

We do not ask players to submit information such as their name, email address, or phone number to create an account. However, third-party services integrated into the Game may process information automatically when you use their features.

Device and technical information

Google services used by the Game may process information such as device type and model, operating system, IP address, advertising identifiers, app version, advertising interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. The information processed can depend on your device settings, the service feature being used, and applicable requirements.

Advertising – Google AdMob

Last Platform uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. In connection with providing and measuring ads, preventing fraud, and operating its services, Google AdMob may process device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic information.

Whether ads are personalized or non-personalized may depend on applicable law, consent choices, and device or account settings. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases – Google Play Billing

Last Platform offers optional in-app purchases, including virtual gem packs and a Remove Ads purchase. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payment and transaction information needed to complete and manage purchases. We do not receive or store your full payment card or bank account details.

The Game receives purchase-related information made available through Google Play Billing, such as product and purchase status information, so it can provide the relevant in-game benefits. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

2. Gameplay information and local storage

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device. This may include your high score, gem balance, selected ball, unlocked balls or other game items, and gameplay preferences. This information is used to preserve your progress and provide the Game’s features.

Ordinary gameplay progress is stored locally and is not associated with a player account or a cloud-save account operated by us. Locally stored information may be deleted if you clear the Game’s app data or uninstall the Game.

3. How information is used

Information processed through the Game and its third-party services may be used to:

  • provide and maintain gameplay functionality;
  • display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements;
  • provide rewards associated with rewarded advertisements;
  • process purchases and provide the corresponding in-game benefits;
  • save gameplay progress and preferences locally;
  • help detect fraud, abuse, and technical problems; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google in connection with the third-party services described in this Policy:

  • Google AdMob provides banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertising.
  • Google Play Billing processes in-app purchases, including gem packs and Remove Ads.

These services process information under their own terms and privacy practices. For details, review Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Data storage and security

Gameplay progress described in this Policy is stored locally on your device. Google may process advertising and transaction information through its services. We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay information generally remains on your device until you clear the Game’s app data or uninstall the Game. Google retains information processed through its services in accordance with its own policies, settings, and applicable legal requirements.

For privacy questions about the Game, contact us at srmstudio14@gmail.com. Requests about information processed directly by Google may also need to be made to Google through its privacy tools or support channels.

7. Children’s privacy

Last Platform is intended for a general gaming audience aged 13 and older and is not specifically designed for children under 13. The Game does not require account registration or knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly to us.

Third-party services integrated into the Game may process device and advertising-related information as described in this Policy. Parents or guardians with questions or concerns about privacy in connection with the Game may contact us at srmstudio14@gmail.com.

8. Third-party privacy information

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage users to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Terms of Service

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Game, the services it uses, our practices, or applicable requirements. We will publish the updated Policy on this webpage and revise the “Last updated” date above.
